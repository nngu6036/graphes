# Joint typed-DH-VAE + soft bond-category diffusion

## Scope and entry points

This is a new attributed model family, selected by `joint_typed_degree.enabled: true`
and `attributed_predictor.type: joint_typed_soft_edge`. The existing
`train_attributed_grapher.py` and `run_attributed_grapher.py` dispatch to it. Their
legacy attributed spectral/graphlet path and the generic joint-degree models are
unchanged. The new family requires a new checkpoint; generic GraphER and legacy
attributed predictor weights are not silently loaded into this architecture.

Configs:
- `configs/experiments/grapher/qm9_attributed_joint_typed_edge.yaml`
- `configs/experiments/grapher/zinc_attributed_joint_typed_edge.yaml`

Supported molecular representation: integer `atomic_num` node categories,
`bond_type` edge categories, and implicit hydrogens, matching the repository's
processed QM9/ZINC codec. No-bond is a separate class at index 0; the remaining
classes follow the configured `edge_categories` order. The supplied configs use
single/double/triple bonds. Explicit nonzero formal charges are rejected rather
than silently discarded: modeling those as supplied attributes would require
extending the typed signature. Stereo, explicit hydrogen state and arbitrary
categorical node attributes are not generated by this family.

## Model and invariants

The prior models complete signatures tau_v = (atomic_num_v, d_v,single,
d_v,double, d_v,triple), not independent atom and bond marginals. A signature
multiset is sampled once and realized by the existing typed backtracking
constructor. Atom categories and indexed typed degrees remain fixed during hard
rewiring. Therefore ordinary degrees, global bond-category counts, and weighted
valence also remain fixed. No hard atom-type diffusion is performed.

The embedded typed-DH-VAE receives the ACTUAL normalized signature histogram and
node count. Its encoder hidden state, posterior mean, signature-decoder hidden
state, decoded signature probabilities, and expected typed incidence are fused
into the denoiser. Structural losses have a differentiable path into the degree
encoder and signature decoder. The signature-output layer also receives these
gradients through its probabilities (except in degenerate one-class support).
The conditioning path uses the posterior mean in training and inference; the
VAE objective uses reparameterized posterior samples and its learned conditional
prior, as in the supplied typed VAE.

Node inputs are fixed atom embeddings plus exact typed degrees. Symmetric dense
edge-message-passing layers consume current noisy bond logits and source bond
logits. Pair features use symmetric endpoint operations, not node-index
positional embeddings. A separate rank-indexed spectral Transformer provides
invariant graph context; spectral ranks are eigenvalue positions, not node IDs.
Optional outputs are topology and bond-order-weighted Laplacian spectra,
clustering histograms, and the ordinary-topology ORCA-15 mean node-orbit vector.
Clustering/orbit are currently TOPOLOGY-ONLY auxiliary summaries, not attributed
graphlet descriptors. Attributed supervision is supplied by the all-pairs bond
prediction conditioned on atom types and typed degrees. Cycle heads are not
included in this focused implementation.

The orbit head reuses the generic model's nonnegative parameterization of nine
necessary ordinary-degree/orbit identities. This does not enforce all typed graph
realizability constraints, nor joint compatibility of independently predicted
summaries.

## Soft-category diffusion: exact definition

For K categories and a hard one-hot pair label e, define p=(1-epsilon)e+epsilon/K
and z=log(p)-mean(log(p)). Endpoints z_src and z_clean use the same smoothing
(default 0.01). At time t in [0,1], training samples

    z_t = (1-t) z_src + t z_clean + sigma sqrt(t(1-t)) epsilon_centered.

One Gaussian vector is sampled per UNORDERED pair, centered across categories,
and mirrored. Thus the class covariance is I - 11'/K. Diagonal and padded logits
are zero; their probabilities are explicitly no-bond. Losses exclude diagonal,
padded pairs, and duplicate lower-triangle entries. The model predicts centered
clean endpoint logits, not directly sampled categorical adjacency.

This is continuous Gaussian diffusion/bridging in a soft categorical embedding.
It is NOT an independent categorical mutation chain on valid molecules. There
are no eigenvector or atom-type perturbations. The optional two spectral
channels are diffused simultaneously in mean-degree-normalized units. Spectral
noise has zero sum on nonzero-eigenvalue slots and keeps lambda_1=0. Noisy
coordinates are not sorted/clipped into a putative graph; they may be negative.
Only predicted endpoint spectra are positive/ordered/trace-constrained.

## Training alignment and losses

For each clean training graph, construct a source with exactly the same INDEXED
signatures (`randomize_assignment=False` during endpoint construction). Apply a
shared random node permutation to both graphs before forming each training
batch. The source/target labels are never independently relabeled. We never
sample a different invariant and pair it with the old target adjacency.

Target-derived endpoint construction preserves the prepared bond labels and
does not apply `constructor.max_ordinary_degree` or
`constructor.max_weighted_valence`. Canonical QM9 preparation uses
`sanitize_sdf: false`, so a stored graph can exceed that generation envelope
(for example, nitrogen with a bond-order sum of five). Sanitizing or changing
bonds here would change the supervised typed signatures. Training and paired
diagnostics therefore reconstruct those records as stored, enforcing simple
connected topology and exact indexed typed degrees, including degree five when
present in a prepared target. The simple-graph bound `degree < num_nodes` still
applies. The endpoint policy is logged and recorded in `run_config.json`,
`report.json`, and endpoint cache diagnostics. Learned/empirical unconditional
generation still applies its configured degree/valence caps and RDKit validity filters.

Vocabulary support comes only from training or from an explicitly supplied
pretrained typed prior. Unknown validation signatures fail with a split/index
message. They are not remapped, silently dropped, or added to vocabulary from
held-out data. Size support is also checked. A legacy initializer's empirical
size distribution is replaced with the current effective training subset; no
empirical invariant fallback is used.

Default loss weights:
- all-unordered-pairs category cross-entropy: 1.0;
- clean centered-logit squared error: 0.1;
- soft expected typed-degree consistency MSE: 0.1;
- spectrum reconstruction: 0.5;
- clustering histogram squared-CDF error: 0.25;
- ordinary orbit log-count Smooth-L1: 0.5;
- existing typed-VAE objective: 0.01 multiplier.

The typed consistency loss compares sum_j P_ij,r with the known d_i,r (scaled by
n); it is a SOFT regularizer. Exact typed preservation is guaranteed by the hard
constructor/swap checks, not by soft probabilities. Degree reconstruction uses
the existing node-count, signature-histogram, typed-incidence and latent-prior
losses. These initial weights are not validated optima.

Every loss is normalized per graph before averaging graphs. The VAE loss is
computed once per graph minibatch, while structural loss is averaged over
`edge_diffusion.views_per_graph` independently sampled bridge states. Structural
view gradients are accumulated separately to avoid storing all views' backward
graphs in memory. Ten percent of training times explicitly cover t=0/t=1;
remaining times are uniform. Fixed validation RNG streams do not perturb the
training RNG. The default typed prior is warm-started, frozen for 10 epochs, then
jointly fine-tuned at a smaller learning rate. Set `trainable: false` for a
permanently frozen-prior control.

HH/typed construction, integer histogram sampling, chemical filtering, and hard
swap selection are outside automatic differentiation. This is joint neural
training, not gradient propagation through combinatorial realization.

Endpoint counting is lazy with a bounded RAM cache and optional JSON-valued
SQLite cache. Cache keys include target graph contents, constructor settings,
seed and summary definitions. There is no hidden validation-to-train fallback.
Full QM9/ZINC endpoint construction/counting has not been benchmarked here.

## Sampling and hard graph realization

This family deliberately uses `bridge_then_rewire`:

1. Sample a typed invariant using the selected joint checkpoint's embedded VAE.
2. Realize a typed source (or resample after declared failures).
3. Starting at source logits and source spectra, evolve an independent SOFT
   bridge state for 32 steps. At each step predict a clean endpoint.
4. Freeze the sampled endpoint bond probabilities and predicted summaries.
5. Refine the typed source using legal same-bond-type double-edge swaps.

For t<s, the implemented soft update is

    z_s = z_t + (s-t)/(1-t) (z_clean_hat-z_t)
          + sigma sqrt((s-t)(1-s)/(1-t)) epsilon_centered.

For a KNOWN endpoint this is the Gaussian bridge conditional. Replacing it with
a learned estimate at each finite step is an approximate sampler, not a claim of
exact data-distribution sampling, a solved Schroedinger bridge, or exact graph
reconstruction. At the last step variance vanishes; earlier steps supply
stochasticity. A terminal prediction supplies auxiliary summaries. Pair
probabilities are obtained from the sampled endpoint logits. Source-only,
artificial-bridge and full soft-rollout diagnostics are separate.

The hard graph is NOT repeatedly converted back into the diffusion state. This
avoids the old mismatch of treating an actual rewired graph as if it were an
artificial noisy bridge sample. It is a new generation strategy, not an
assertion that old spectral-only checkpoints implement this sampler.

The edge energy includes ALL unordered pairs:

    E_edge(G) = -mean_{i<j} log P_ij,category(G_ij).

In particular, removing an edge changes its label to no-bond; scoring only
present edges would omit that term and is not used. Other distances are
clustering-histogram W1 and orbit log-RMSE. Default generation weights are
edge=1.0, clustering=0.25, orbit=1.0. Spectral distance is not scored.

For each active component, its scale is max(initial discrepancy, epsilon), fixed
for the entire graph. Weighted discrepancy reductions select greedy swaps. This
`normalization: initial` makes the complete frozen-target energy unchanging
between decisions; it differs from the older refiner's step-dependent relative
normalization. `normalization: none` uses raw discrepancies. Neither requires
every individual component to improve on an accepted move.

The hard search has 1024 proposal orientations / at most 256 valid candidates
per decision and 32 accepted swaps maximum. It considers two reconnection
orientations for pairs of bonds of the SAME category, rejects existing edges,
self-loops, disconnection, revisited states, and (by default) RDKit-invalid
candidates. Reported stops refer to the SAMPLED candidate set, not a proof of a
local/global optimum. Same-type moves may not connect all typed realizations.

Generation applies strict raw RDKit source filtering by default. It does not
repair bond orders, change atoms, or infer projected formal charges. This can
reject invariant draws (including some charged species represented by the old
codec without charge) and biases the returned distribution; histogram draws,
constructor failures and chemical source rejection counts are reported. The
training source constructor only enforces combinatorial typed feasibility;
source filtering at generation is an additional restriction. This possible
conditioning shift is explicit, not assumed absent. Chemical sanitization does
not establish stability, synthesizability, or complete chemistry semantics.

Independent source, soft-trajectory and rewiring RNG streams prevent differences
in rewiring cost/weights from changing later source draws. Changing the joint
checkpoint can of course change its sampled invariant distribution.

## Checkpoints and provenance

Snapshots are independent for `best_joint`, `best_edges`, `best_histogram`,
`best_orbit`, and `last` (summary selections only when their head is enabled).
Best selection is post-unfreeze for joint runs; all epochs for permanently
frozen priors. Each folder contains joint `checkpoint.pt`, matching
`degree_checkpoint.pt`, and `selection.json`. The root checkpoint pair aliases
best_joint. `checkpoint_registry.json` stores epochs and hashes. These are
inference snapshots without optimizer/RNG state for exact resumption.

New checkpoints are tensor/primitive payloads loaded with `weights_only=True`.
Do not load untrusted original graph pickle splits. The full processed
train/validation/test file fingerprints are recorded; generation refuses a
mismatch. Legacy initializer provenance is not invented: its file hash is
recorded, but a historical training fingerprint is not available when absent
from that initializer. Existing directories with artifacts are not overwritten.
A failed/incomplete generation writes `partial_*.pkl`; only a complete run
publishes `molecular_graphs.pkl`. Failures are never silently replaced by
empirical invariants.

## QM9 commands

Apply the focused patch from the repository root, keeping its relative paths.
The default config expects the existing typed (NOT ordinary) degree checkpoint:

    outputs/degree_generators/qm9_typed/seed_42/checkpoint.pt

To produce it using the existing standalone trainer when needed:

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/train_degree_generator.py \
  --config configs/experiments/dhvae/qm9_typed.yaml
```

Joint training (new pair model, warm-started typed prior):

```bash
CFG=configs/experiments/grapher/qm9_attributed_joint_typed_edge.yaml
TRAIN=outputs/attributed_grapher/qm9_joint_typed_edge/seed_42
GEN=outputs/attributed_generation/qm9_joint_typed_edge/seed_42

PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/train_attributed_grapher.py \
  --config "$CFG" --output-dir "$TRAIN" --seed 42 --device gpu

PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/inspect_joint_typed_checkpoints.py \
  --training-dir "$TRAIN" --verify
```

A bounded smoke run can use a fresh output directory and
`--epochs 2 --max-train-graphs 128 --max-val-graphs 32
--set joint_typed_degree.freeze_epochs=0`. This still uses the typed initializer.
To explicitly train the prior from scratch, ALSO set
`--set joint_typed_degree.initialize_degree_checkpoint=null`; unseen validation
signatures in a small training subset will raise rather than leak vocabulary.

Source-only and soft-rollout diagnostics (paired known validation targets):

```bash
for MODE in source rollout; do
  PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/diagnose_joint_typed_edge.py \
    --config "$CFG" --checkpoint "$TRAIN/checkpoint.pt" \
    --split val --mode "$MODE" --max-graphs 64 --samples-per-graph 1 \
    --seed 42 --device gpu --json-out "$TRAIN/diagnostic_${MODE}_val.json"
done
```

`--mode bridge` evaluates artificial training-like bridge inputs. `--mode
rollout --rewire` additionally tests paired hard realization. Edge accuracy is a
label-aligned diagnostic, not a molecular isomorphism score. Watch bond-only
accuracy along with all-pairs accuracy: no-bond pairs can dominate the latter.
Soft typed-degree residuals are not hard constraint-violation rates.

Generation uses the embedded prior, not an external degree checkpoint:

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/run_attributed_grapher.py \
  --config "$CFG" --checkpoint "$TRAIN/checkpoint.pt" \
  --output-dir "$GEN" --num-generate 64 --seed 42 --device gpu
```

Saved complete outputs include `coarse_graphs.pkl`, `molecular_graphs.pkl`,
`soft_endpoints.pkl`, and `report.json`. Source sampling attempts and hard-graph
invariant checks are separate from soft sampling steps and prediction calls.

Use the unchanged molecule evaluator; validation is explicit in its CLI (the
standalone evaluator does not read this experiment YAML):

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/evaluate_generated_molecules.py \
  --generated-graphs "$GEN/molecular_graphs.pkl" \
  --dataset qm9_attributed --reference-split val --train-split train \
  --metric-molecule-source raw_valid \
  --output-dir "$GEN/evaluation_val" --require-fcd
```

FCD needs the existing ChemNet/FCD backend and NSPDK uses the existing EDeN
backend. Missing dependencies are not silently treated as successful metrics.
A smoke-only evaluation can use `--skip-fcd --nspdk-backend proxy`, but those
outputs must be labeled as proxy/no-FCD, not benchmark-compatible results.
After fixing the experiment, use `--reference-split test` and a separate
`evaluation_test` directory. This patch does not change validity/NSPDK/FCD
definitions or add corrected molecules to the raw-valid subset.

## Controlled ablations

- Freeze the pretrained typed prior for an otherwise identical training run:
  `--set joint_typed_degree.trainable=false` (use a new TRAIN directory).
- Set `generation.invariant_source=train_empirical` for supplied training typed
  invariants. Do not call this unconditional learned-prior generation.
- With one trained checkpoint, set weights `{edge:1, clustering:0, orbit:0}` to
  isolate pair scoring; or `{edge:0, clustering:0.25, orbit:1}` for summary scoring.
  Soft bridge sampling remains the same; these are scoring-only ablations.
- Disable spectral inputs/supervision in a NEW training run with
  `edge_diffusion.spectral_enabled=false` and loss weight `spectrum=0`.
  A checkpoint's spectral/noise/smoothing semantics cannot be switched at generation.
- Compare `best_edges` to `best_joint` using its own embedded prior and matching
  degree export. Different checkpoints need not produce the same source batch.

## Validation performed

See JOINT_TYPED_EDGE_TEST_RESULTS.txt. Tests cover symmetric centered Gaussian
noise and conditional moments, endpoints/masks, all-pairs/no-bond scoring,
permutation equivariance, padding independence, graph-balanced losses,
gradients into typed encoder and decoder, frozen-prior behavior, safe checkpoint
round trips, matching per-selection exports, typed/weighted-valence-preserving
mixed-bond swaps, RNG stream independence, cached alignment, unsupported config
errors, CLI dispatch, source/bridge/rollout diagnostics, and a small molecule
evaluation with explicitly labeled proxy NSPDK and no FCD.

No full QM9/ZINC training, GPU execution, full-scale runtime study, or generated
molecule quality claim is implied by those CPU tests.
